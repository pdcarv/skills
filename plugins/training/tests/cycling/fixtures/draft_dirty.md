# Strength block rationale

Heavy strength training raises FTP by 7% over 12 weeks (Coggan et al., 2019,
J Appl Physiol), and Seiler (2013) showed the polarized model produces a 9.4%
improvement in trained cyclists. Confidence Level 5.

Critical power is 285 W. Indoor LT2 is 280 W and the athlete weighs 75 kg,
giving 3.73 W/kg. Lactate at threshold was 47 mmol/L.

The authors concluded that "heavy strength training can improve cycling
performance in endurance cyclists and this improvement may be due to efficiency."

They also stated it was a "significant effect on cycling efficiency" across the
pooled sample.
