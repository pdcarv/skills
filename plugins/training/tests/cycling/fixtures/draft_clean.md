# Strength block rationale

The 2025 meta-analysis (Llanos-Lagos et al., 2025) pooled 17 studies and 262
cyclists and found a small effect on cycling efficiency (ES 0.353, p = 0.012).
Cohort mean mass was 72.28 kg, which is why transfer to this athlete is rated
partial. [[L-0001 L3]]

Durability appears independent of threshold: the 20-minute decay was 10.1% in a
20-rider amateur cohort. [[L-0002 L2]]

Critical power resolves to 250.0 W with a W-prime of 36000 J and a subset spread
of 8.4 W.

Indoor LT2 remains 260 W.

Session count for the block is 24 sessions.
