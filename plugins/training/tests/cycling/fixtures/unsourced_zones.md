Outdoor HR sits ~3 bpm below indoor at the same metabolic intensity — thermal
load, not fitness.

Your Z3 is narrow — 24 W outdoors. That's a direct consequence of LT1/LT2 at
0.83. A high ratio compresses the tempo band, which means the "grey zone"
between endurance and sub-threshold is easy to fall into by accident. Discipline
matters more for you than for a rider with a 0.76 ratio: 228 W is
endurance-adjacent, 248 W is a quality session, and there is very little in
between.

Set the platform FTP to 250 W and use the outdoor column for its zone display,
since that's where the majority of your quality work sits.

The 6-minute step test — 160/180/200/220/240/260 W, resting sample first, fixed
85–90 rpm — resolves LT1, LT2 and the full HR-power map in one session.
